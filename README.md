## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the most important challenges faced by subscription-based businesses.

This project presents an **end-to-end Customer Churn Analysis solution** designed to understand customer behaviour, identify churn patterns, analyze customer segments, and uncover factors associated with customer attrition.

The project combines **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to transform customer data into interactive business intelligence.

The analysis focuses on:

* 👥 Customer behaviour
* 📉 Customer churn
* 📊 Customer demographics
* 💳 Payment methods
* 📦 Services & subscriptions
* 💰 Billing behaviour
* 📄 Contract types
* 🛠️ Technical support
* 🔐 Online security
* 📱 Device protection

> **Goal:** Identify important churn patterns and translate customer data into actionable insights that can support customer retention strategies.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

The project aims to answer key business questions such as:

* What percentage of customers are churning?
* How many customers are retained versus churned?
* Which contract types are associated with higher churn?
* Does internet service type influence churn?
* How does payment method relate to customer churn?
* Are senior citizens more likely to churn?
* How do gender, partners and dependents relate to churn?
* Does TechSupport influence customer retention?
* Does OnlineSecurity affect churn behaviour?
* How does DeviceProtection relate to churn?
* What customer characteristics should businesses consider when designing retention strategies?

# 💡 Key Business Insights

### 🔴 1. Churn is a Significant Business Issue

The overall churn rate is **26.58%**, while **73.42% of customers are retained**. This makes customer retention an important area for business improvement.

### 📄 2. Contract Type Matters

The dashboard compares churn across **Monthly, One Year and Two Year contracts**, allowing businesses to identify contract groups with higher customer attrition.

### 🌐 3. Internet Service Can Be Evaluated as a Churn Driver

Churn is segmented across **DSL, Fiber Optic and No Internet Service**, enabling comparison of customer retention behaviour across service types.

### 🛠️ 4. Customer Support & Services Matter

The analysis evaluates **TechSupport, OnlineSecurity and DeviceProtection** against churn, providing a basis for understanding how service adoption relates to customer retention.

### 💳 5. Payment Behaviour Provides Additional Customer Insight

Customer churn is also analysed across payment methods including **Electronic Check, Mailed Check, Bank Transfer and Credit Card**.

---

# 💼 Business Recommendations

### 1. 🎯 Focus on High-Risk Customer Segments

Identify customer groups with higher churn rates and design targeted retention campaigns instead of using a one-size-fits-all strategy.

### 2. 📄 Encourage Longer-Term Contracts

Analyse high-churn contract groups and consider incentives that encourage customers to move toward longer-term plans where appropriate.

### 3. 🛠️ Strengthen Support Services

Evaluate the relationship between TechSupport, OnlineSecurity and DeviceProtection adoption and churn to determine whether service bundles can improve retention.

### 4. 💳 Monitor Payment-Related Churn

Investigate customer experience and friction associated with different payment methods and improve payment convenience where required.

### 5. 👥 Personalize Retention Strategies

Use demographic and customer-service characteristics to develop more personalized customer retention campaigns.
